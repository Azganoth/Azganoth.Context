# Profile

## Background

- I am Brazilian and a trans woman.
- My path into programming began with JavaScript for Atom plugins, Lua for World of Warcraft addons, and Python in college before I moved into web development around 2019.

## Interests

- I am drawn to science, philosophy, social theory, media criticism, and narrative-heavy games.
- I enjoy understanding the underlying mechanics of things, from biology and cosmology to institutions, ideologies, and fictional worlds.
- In art and media, I value form as part of the meaning. Environmental storytelling, music, sound design, staging, mechanics, spaces, objects, and other nonverbal elements can matter to me as much as explicit exposition.
- My musical taste is not organized around a single genre. I am especially drawn to work where sonic craft and conceptual meaning develop together through experimentation, unusual structure, genre mixing, allegory, or social commentary.
- I appreciate works that trust the audience to reconstruct meaning rather than explaining everything directly.
- *Divinity: Original Sin 2* is a narrative touchstone for me because it combines systemic player agency, emotionally consequential companion stories, music, and the possibility of rejecting oppressive institutions.
- *The Witcher 3* and its music are emotionally significant touchstones tied to my adolescence, entering college, and finding moments of joy during a difficult period. Revisiting them can evoke strong nostalgia.
- In fiction, I often resonate with characters who combine specialized competence and personal agency with a clear view of the systems constraining them. I also respond strongly to pragmatic, emotionally honest leadership that preserves others' agency and leads through action rather than empty idealism.
- I appreciate speculative fiction that takes genuinely nonhuman minds seriously and does not assume human-style individuality is the natural endpoint of intelligence.

## Worldview and values

- I am agnostic and broadly materialist, with perspectives influenced by historical and dialectical materialism and Foucauldian analysis.
- I distinguish contradictions from paradoxes. In dialectical analysis, contradictions within a system can be forces that drive historical change rather than logical dead ends.
- I tend to understand beliefs and institutions through their history, material conditions, power relations, and social function. I can recognize that a belief helps someone without accepting its truth claims.
- I am skeptical of narratives that resolve systemic harm by replacing leaders while leaving the underlying institutions intact. I am more interested in whether the structures and incentives themselves can be changed.
- I am skeptical that peaceful appeals alone can overcome entrenched systemic power. When discussing resistance, take seriously the role of coercive power and the material limits of moral persuasion.
- I am interested in scientific and material accounts of human behavior, including how biology and environment complicate ideas of free will, blame, merit, and punishment.
- I value autonomy, self-determination, and resistance to oppressive power. Empathy for a person does not require conceding their ideology.
